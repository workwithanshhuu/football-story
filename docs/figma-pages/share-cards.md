# Share Cards

Frames: `1080 x 1920` story and `1200 x 630` link preview  
Traceability: `FR-4.7-04`, `FR-4.8-02`, `FR-4.11-02`, `FR-4.15-01` through `FR-4.15-06`

## Match Scorecard

Stadium or pitch texture, solid readable content zone, footArena mark, teams, final score, venue, date, highlights, MVP badge, and deep link.

## Player Performance

Player identity, position, match, scoreline, goals, assists, and position-specific action tally. Placeholder identity uses first name plus shirt number only.

## Career Milestone

Large milestone number, supporting stat, matches count, player identity, footArena mark, and share/deep-link CTA.

## Special Labels

Use `ABANDONED - PARTIAL` for abandoned matches, `NO RESULT` when no winning scoreline exists, and `CORRECTED` with correction count for corrected scorecards.
